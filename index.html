01 — About

Physics made fun

We're a club at Amador Valley High School for anyone curious about the mysteries of the universe, whether you're a student in AP Physics C or just someone who is just starting out.

We usually have demos or other fun activities at our meetings, such as presentations by guest speakers, competition prep, and teaching things in physics the school doesn't teach you.

  • MeetsEvery other Thursday at lunch
  • WhereRoom R210 (Mrs. Barnett's classroom)
  • Open toEveryone (No physics background required!)

02 — What we do

Four main things:

Biweekly meetings

Demos, fun problems, and presentations. We've had wind tunnels, hovercrafts, the physics of sports, video games, and much, much more.

Competitions

We offer prep for Physics Bowl and the F=ma exam, as well as the Berkeley Physics Tournament (new!).

Register for the AAPT Photo Contest

Guest talks

Researchers, engineers, and others on what physics actually looks like after high school.

Food

We will most likely have snacks at our meetings.

03 — Projects

Things members have worked on...

Simulations

Modeling things computationally

The three-body problem, a wormhole, a black hole, Project Hail Mary's time dilation, and the double pendulum

Experiments

The double slit experiment

See quantum mechanics in action, when light behaves like a wave.

Theory

Mathematics behind physics

Newton's laws of motion, the conservation of energy, electricity and magnetism, and so much more.
